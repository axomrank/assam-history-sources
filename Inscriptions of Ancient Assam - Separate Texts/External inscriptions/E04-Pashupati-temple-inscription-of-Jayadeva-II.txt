Pashupati temple inscription of Jayadeva II

Date: 737 CE; correction noted by Sharma
Ruler or persons named: Jayadeva II; Rajyamati and Harshadeva
Place: Kathmandu, Nepal

SUMMARY
Records Jayadeva’s marriage to Rajyamati, daughter of Harshadeva, and describes her family through Bhagadatta ancestry.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
Harshadeva’s identification with the Kamarupa ruler connects the inscription to Assam. This is not a local inscription.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.15–0.16, supplied PDF p. 32. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
