Belava copper plates

Date: Twelfth century CE
Ruler or persons named: Bhojavarman; Jatavarman praised
Place: Belava, Bangladesh

SUMMARY
A poetic passage about Jatavarman has been read as referring to the humiliation of Kamarupa.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
Sharma questions that interpretation: a play on Kama and rupa may concern beauty rather than a political conquest.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.16–0.17, supplied PDF p. 33. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
