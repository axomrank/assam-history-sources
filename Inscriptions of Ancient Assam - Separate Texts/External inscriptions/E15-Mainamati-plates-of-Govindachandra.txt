Mainamati plates of Govindachandra

Date: Eleventh century CE
Ruler or persons named: Govindachandra; Kalyanachandra praised
Place: Mainamati, Bangladesh

SUMMARY
Praises Kalyanachandra through a passage about Lauhitya and the tears of Mleccha women.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
The implied association with Kamarupa and the historical scale of the campaign require interpretation.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.18, supplied PDF p. 35. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
