Paschimbhag copper plates

Date: Tenth century CE
Ruler or persons named: Srichandra
Place: Paschimbhag, Sylhet region, Bangladesh

SUMMARY
The eulogy claims a Kamarupa campaign and mentions Lauhitya and Pushpabhadra.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
An external campaign claim must not be converted automatically into a permanent frontier.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.16, supplied PDF p. 33. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
