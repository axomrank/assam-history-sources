Rajghat inscription of Bhimadeva

Date: Mid-twelfth century context, approximately 1144–1161
Ruler or persons named: Bhimadeva, minister of Madanapala
Place: Rajghat; precise provenance to confirm in original edition

SUMMARY
Praises Bhimadeva for saving Gauda or Varendra from an onslaught metaphorically described as waters or kings of Rayari’s lineage.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
Poetic identification and political circumstances need the original edition. Outside the requested date range.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.18, supplied PDF p. 35. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
