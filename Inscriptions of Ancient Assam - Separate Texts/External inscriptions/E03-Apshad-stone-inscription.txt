Apshad stone inscription

Date: Seventh century CE; Sharma gives approximately 672
Ruler or persons named: Adityasena; Mahasenagupta and Susthitavarman named
Place: Apshad / Aphsad, Bihar

SUMMARY
Praises Mahasenagupta’s defeat of Susthitavarman on the banks of the Brahmaputra.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
Identification of Susthitavarman with the Kamarupa ruler is a historical interpretation.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.15, supplied PDF p. 32. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
