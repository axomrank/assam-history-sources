Silimpur stone slab inscription

Date: Late eleventh or early twelfth century; exact placement not fixed here
Ruler or persons named: Jayapaladeva
Place: Silimpur, Bogra region, Bangladesh

SUMMARY
Records a tulapurusha charitable offering associated with Jayapaladeva.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
Jayapala’s political and chronological identification needs the original edition. Do not place it securely before 1100.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.17, supplied PDF p. 34. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
