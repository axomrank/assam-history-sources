Madhainagar copper plates

Date: Twelfth century CE
Ruler or persons named: Lakshmanasena
Place: Madhainagar, Bangladesh

SUMMARY
Contains a claim concerning supremacy over a Kamarupa king.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
Identifying the unnamed ruler with Rayarideva is a scholarly proposal. Outside the 350–1100 core.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.17, supplied PDF p. 34. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
