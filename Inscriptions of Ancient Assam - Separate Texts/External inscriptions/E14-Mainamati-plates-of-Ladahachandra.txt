Mainamati plates of Ladahachandra

Date: Eleventh century CE
Ruler or persons named: Ladahachandra; Srichandra praised
Place: Mainamati, Bangladesh

SUMMARY
Contains praise of Srichandra using the grief of women of Pragjyotisha as an image of military success.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
The poetic claim is evidence for royal representation and possible conflict, not a detailed campaign report.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.18, supplied PDF p. 35. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
