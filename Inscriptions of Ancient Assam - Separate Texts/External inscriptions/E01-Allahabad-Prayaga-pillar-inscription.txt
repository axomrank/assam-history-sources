Allahabad / Prayaga pillar inscription

Date: Fourth century CE
Ruler or persons named: Samudragupta
Place: Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh

SUMMARY
Names Kamarupa and Davaka among frontier kingdoms in a Gupta imperial eulogy.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
Contemporary outside attestation; claims of submission belong to the imperial praise text.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.15, supplied PDF p. 32. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
