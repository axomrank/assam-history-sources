Samangad plates of Dantidurga

Date: Mid-eighth century CE; exact conversion not re-edited here
Ruler or persons named: Dantidurga; Kirtivarman and Sri Harsha named
Place: Samangad, Maharashtra

SUMMARY
A victory eulogy mentions Sri Harsha among opponents in a passage concerning the Karnataka ruler.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
Sharma proposes an identification with a Kamarupa ruler; the identification is not explicit in the inscription.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.16, supplied PDF p. 33. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
