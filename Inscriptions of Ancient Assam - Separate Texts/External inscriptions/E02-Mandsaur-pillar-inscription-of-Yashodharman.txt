Mandsaur pillar inscription of Yashodharman

Date: Approximately 525–535 CE in Sharma’s broad dating
Ruler or persons named: Yashodharman
Place: Mandsaur, Madhya Pradesh

SUMMARY
Claims an expansive sphere of victory or authority reaching the Brahmaputra.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
A reference to the river is not a local Kamarupa grant or proof of sustained administration there.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.15, supplied PDF p. 32. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
