Bhagalpur grant of Narayanapala

Date: Late ninth or early tenth century CE
Ruler or persons named: Narayanapala; Devapala referenced
Place: Bhagalpur, Bihar

SUMMARY
Contains a dynastic account used to discuss relations between Bengal’s Pala rulers and Kamarupa.

HOW IT RELATES TO KAMARUPA
Narrated events may precede the issue of the grant. Bengal Palas and Kamarupa Palas are distinct dynasties.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam, Gauhati University, 1978, introduction p. 0.16, supplied PDF p. 33. This summary follows Sharma’s notice; the full external edition has not been re-edited here.
