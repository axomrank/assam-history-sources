Uttarbarbil Howraghat Copper Plates Balavarman III

Date: Fifth regnal year of Balavarman III; late ninth century CE.
Ruler: Balavarman III, Salastambha line.
Place: Uttarbarbil in the Howraghat area; also called the Howraghat plates.

SUMMARY
A copper-plate grant records a donation to a Brahmin, with royal genealogy, praise, the beneficiary’s background, the occasion of the gift and the boundaries of the donated land. Its wording closely resembles the Nowgong charter.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Documents Balavarman III’s authority and religious patronage. Uttarbarbil and Howraghat are alternative names for this record, not two separate grants. Its regnal year can be compared with his eighth-year and thirteenth-year charters.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 181-195.
