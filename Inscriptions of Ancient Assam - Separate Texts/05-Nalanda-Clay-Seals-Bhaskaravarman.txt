Nalanda Clay Seals Bhaskaravarman

Date: Seventh century CE; excavated fragments were recovered in 1917–18 and 1927–28.
Ruler: Bhaskaravarman, Varman dynasty.
Place: Nalanda, Bihar, outside Assam.

SUMMARY
Fragmentary clay seals recovered at Nalanda preserve a royal genealogy substantially corresponding to the seal of the Dubi charter. Sharma compares the fragments and reconstructs their wording with help from parallel records.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
They independently attest the presence of Bhaskaravarman’s royal seal material at Nalanda and corroborate parts of his genealogy. They are evidence of connections beyond Assam; the seals alone do not establish political rule over Nalanda.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 89-91.
