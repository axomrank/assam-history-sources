Gachtal pillar inscription

Date: Later medieval; 1227 and later datings occur in the literature
Ruler or person named: Not established here
Place: Gachtal, Nagaon

SUMMARY
A pillar record distinct from Gopalavarman’s copper-plate charter.

LIMITS AND HISTORICAL SCOPE
The date and reading need the original edition. It is excluded from the 350–1100 core regardless of the competing later dates.
This record is later than the 350–1100 CE focus. It is retained as context, not counted in that period.
