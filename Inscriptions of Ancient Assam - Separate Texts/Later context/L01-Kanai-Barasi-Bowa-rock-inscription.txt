Kanai Barasi Bowa rock inscription

Date: Saka 1127; conventionally 1206 CE
Ruler or person named: Local ruler not named
Place: North Guwahati

SUMMARY
Records the destruction of Turks entering Kamarupa.

LIMITS AND HISTORICAL SCOPE
Does not itself name Prithu or Bakhtiyar Khalji. The Saka/CE conversion and the exact day should be checked in the original edition.
This record is later than the 350–1100 CE focus. It is retained as context, not counted in that period.
