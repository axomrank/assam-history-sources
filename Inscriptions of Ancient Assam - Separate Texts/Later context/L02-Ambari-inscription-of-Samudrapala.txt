Ambari inscription of Samudrapala

Date: Thirteenth century; 1232 CE proposed in Sanyal’s inventory
Ruler or person named: Samudrapala
Place: Ambari, Guwahati

SUMMARY
Inscribed stone associated with Samudrapala.

LIMITS AND HISTORICAL SCOPE
The exact date is not re-edited here. Earlier broad twelfth–thirteenth placement is not used to include it before 1100.
This record is later than the 350–1100 CE focus. It is retained as context, not counted in that period.
