Suwalkuchi Copper Plates Ratnapala

Date: Twenty-sixth regnal year of Ratnapala; approximately 1036 CE as headed in this edition.
Ruler: Ratnapala, Kamarupa Pala dynasty.
Place: Suwalkuchi or Sualkuchi, Kamrup.

SUMMARY
The charter records a Brahmin land grant, with royal genealogy, the beneficiary’s ancestry, privileges and detailed boundaries. Boundary markers include trees, a brick field and neighbouring holdings. The twenty-sixth regnal year follows the Bargaon charter’s twenty-fifth year.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Useful for the dynasty’s succession, rural geography and the documentary form of royal grants. Its approximate CE date depends on the accession chronology adopted.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 227-232.
