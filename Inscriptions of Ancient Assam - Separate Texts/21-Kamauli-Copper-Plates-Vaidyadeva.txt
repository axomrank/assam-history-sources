Kamauli Copper Plates Vaidyadeva

Date: Fourth regnal year of Vaidyadeva; approximately 1142 CE in Sharma’s edition.
Ruler: Vaidyadeva.
Place: Kamauli near Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh; discovered in 1891.

SUMMARY
The charter grants the villages Santipataka and Mandara in the Kamarupa administrative hierarchy to the Brahmin Sridhara. It presents Vaidyadeva’s genealogy and achievements and describes his political connections with the Bengal Pala court. A Ganesha emblem appears on the seal.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
A key source for political developments following the Kamarupa Palas and for connections between Assam and Bengal. The findspot near Varanasi is distinct from the territory described in the grant.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 328-345.
