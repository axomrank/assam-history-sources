Nidhanpur Copper Plates Bhaskaravarman

Date: Approximately 620–643 CE in Sharma’s dating.
Ruler: Bhaskaravarman; the earlier grant is attributed to Bhutivarman.
Place: Nidhanpur in Sylhet, now Bangladesh; issued from Karnasuvarna.

SUMMARY
The charter renews an earlier land grant whose documentary record had been lost or destroyed by fire. It describes land in the Mayurasalmala agrahara, lists numerous Brahmin beneficiaries with their religious and genealogical affiliations, allocates shares and sets out boundaries and grant conditions.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
One of the richest sources for Varman genealogy, Brahmin settlement, land administration and historical geography. Its place of issue is important for Bhaskaravarman’s political activity beyond the Brahmaputra valley. The long beneficiary list is especially valuable for social history.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 92-135.
