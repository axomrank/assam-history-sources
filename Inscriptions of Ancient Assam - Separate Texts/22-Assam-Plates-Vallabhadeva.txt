Assam Plates Vallabhadeva

Date: 1185 CE.
Ruler: Vallabhadeva; genealogy names Bhaskara, Rayarideva and Udayakarna.
Place: Made known through Tezpur; also called the Tezpur plates of Vallabha.

SUMMARY
A five-plate charter combines royal genealogy and praise with a charitable endowment and its conditions. It includes invocations to Ganesha and Vishnu. Sharma discusses the place names and the difficulty of fitting Vallabhadeva and his ancestors into the better-known sequence of Kamarupa rulers.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
An important late-twelfth-century record, but its precise political relationship to Vaidyadeva’s line is uncertain. Sharma notes that the text does not explicitly call Vallabha a king of Kamarupa or clearly identify his governmental seat.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 346-356.
