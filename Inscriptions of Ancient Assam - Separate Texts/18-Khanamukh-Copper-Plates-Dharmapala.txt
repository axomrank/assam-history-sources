Khanamukh Copper Plates Dharmapala

Date: First regnal year of Dharmapala; first half of the twelfth century in this edition’s historical framework.
Ruler: Dharmapala, Kamarupa Pala dynasty; son of Harsapala and Ratna.
Place: Khanamukh in Barbhagia mauza, in the region described as Nowgong district.

SUMMARY
A three-plate charter records a Brahmin grant assessed as yielding 6,000 units of paddy. It gives the royal genealogy, the beneficiary’s background and detailed boundaries using fields, trees, streams, neighbouring grants and settlements. Sharma discusses administrative terms and the geographical information preserved in it.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
A major source for the later Pala succession and rural administration. Its first-regnal-year date provides a relative chronological anchor, but converting it to an exact CE year depends on the chronology adopted.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 279-294.
