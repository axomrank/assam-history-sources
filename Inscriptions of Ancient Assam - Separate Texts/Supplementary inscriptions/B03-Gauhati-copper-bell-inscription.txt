Gauhati copper bell inscription

Date: Eighth century CE in published inventories
Ruler or named person: Srikumara
Place: Udalbakra / Narakasurgaon, Guwahati

SUMMARY
An inscribed copper bell preserves the name Srikumara. Its archaeological provenance and religious use make it distinct from the royal copper-plate charters.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Evidence for religious material culture and a named individual outside the main charter corpus.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE
The full edited inscription has not been read in this audit. Do not infer a dynasty, royal succession or a detailed donation from the name alone. NMMA search catalogue reports two inscribed lines. Collect D. Sarma’s edition before expanding the summary.

Source: NMMA SM-ASM-AM-229383; D. Sarma, The Copper Bell Inscription of Srikumara; Sanyal 2014, p.218
