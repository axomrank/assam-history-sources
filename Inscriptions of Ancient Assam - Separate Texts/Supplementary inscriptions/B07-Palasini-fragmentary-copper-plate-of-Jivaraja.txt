Palasini fragmentary copper plate of Jivaraja

Date: Probably early medieval; identification with eighth-century Jivara proposed
Ruler or named person: Maharajadhiraja Sri Jivaraja
Place: Palasini, Nagaon

SUMMARY
The published archaeological discussion identifies a fragmentary land-grant plate naming Sri Jivaraja and a Brahmin from Kamarupa. It cites D. Chutia’s original edition of 1989–90.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Adds a grant outside Sharma’s formal corpus and a possible connection between central Assam and the Deopani image records.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE
This is an edition locator, not a new reading. Identification with Sri Jivara and an eighth-century date are proposals. It is kept outside the securely dated 350–1100 core pending the full edition.

Source: Mrigakhee Saikia (2021), Archaeological Remains of the Kapili-Jamuna Valley of Assam, pp.38–39,44; D. Chutia, Journal of Assam Research Society XXXI (1 & 2), 1989–90, pp.106–114
