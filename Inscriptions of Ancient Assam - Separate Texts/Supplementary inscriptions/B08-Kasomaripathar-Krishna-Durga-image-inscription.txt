Kasomaripathar Krishna-Durga image inscription

Date: Early medieval attribution; precise date not verified
Ruler or named person: Sri Jivara / Jivaraja identification proposed
Place: Kasomaripathar, Golaghat

SUMMARY
Saikia’s discussion cites a Krishna-Durga image inscription in relation to the identification of Sri Jivaraja of the Palasini plate with Sri Jivara of the Deopani record.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
A further inscription to collect when investigating the Dhansiri valley and regional religious images.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE
Only a published reference has been checked. The original reading, date and catalogue identity remain to be verified. It is not counted in the dated core.
