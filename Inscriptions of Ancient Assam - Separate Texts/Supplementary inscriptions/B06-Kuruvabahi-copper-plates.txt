Kuruvabahi copper plates

Date: Ninth century CE, reign of Harjaravarman
Ruler or named person: Harjaravarman
Place: Kuruvabahi, Nagaon region

SUMMARY
A separate charter of Harjaravarman is identified in the scholarly inventory. Its first edition is cited as D. Chutia, Kuruvabahi (Nagaon) Copper Plate Grant of Harjjara(varma)deva, Journal of Assam Research Society, 1981–82.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
An additional charter absent from the 1978 formal corpus, distinct from Hayunthal.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE
The original edition and image were not accessible in this audit. Detailed beneficiary and boundary readings are not supplied.
