Duboroni stone inscription

Date: Approximately 400–500 CE, palaeographic attribution
Ruler or named person: Sri Vasundharavarman
Place: Duboroni, Borpathar, Golaghat; collected about 2 km northwest of the excavated mound

SUMMARY
The excavation report describes a one-line stone inscription measuring 44 × 23 × 8.8 cm, beginning with a trident sign. Its script is compared with Nagajari-Khanikargaon. The reported reading names Sri Vasundharavarman.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Adds early written evidence from the Dhansiri valley. It is documented in a report covering fieldwork well after Sharma’s publication.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE
The report calls him perhaps a regional king and infers a Shaiva affiliation. It does not establish his place in the Guwahati Varman dynasty. A wharf interpretation occurs in later teaching material but is not established by the passage checked here. Discovery date is not equated with the report year.

Source: Archaeological Survey of India, Indian Archaeology 1997–98—A Review (published 2003), printed pp.6,8–9; PDF pp.14,16–17
