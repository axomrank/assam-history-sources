Kaki Surya image inscription

Date: Ninth century CE in published inventories
Ruler or named person: Issuer not established
Place: Kaki, Nagaon

SUMMARY
An inscribed Surya image from Kaki adds an image record to the regional corpus. A dedicated edition is attributed to D. Sarma under the title Inscription on the Surya image from Kaki.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
A source for the material history of solar worship in central Assam.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE
Bibliographic summary only: the complete edition and legible inscription image are still needed. No donor, ruler or grant terms are inferred. The museum has a Surya record from Kaki, but this alone does not establish that it is the same inscribed object.

Source: Kings and Cults in the Land of Kamarupa, notes citing D. Sarma, pp.179–180; compare NMMA object SM-ASM-AM-167411
