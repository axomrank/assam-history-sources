Kaliabor / Dighaligaon copper plates

Date: Ninth century CE
Ruler or named person: Vanamalavarmadeva
Place: Dighaligaon, Kaliabor, Nagaon

SUMMARY
The museum record describes three inscribed plates bound by a ring and elephant seal. It identifies the donor as Vanamalavarmadeva, son and successor of Harjaravarman, and records a gift of a khandalaka of land in Purjeka-pardisa in the southern division.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
A separate Vanamala charter, additional to Tezpur and Parbatiya. The original edition is D. Chutia, Journal of Assam Research Society XXIX (1986–87), pp. 37–52.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE
NMMA’s descriptive paragraph is used; its structured dynasty field incorrectly says Late Ahom Period. No exact CE grant year is asserted here.
