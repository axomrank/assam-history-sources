Guwakuchi Copper Plates Indrapala

Date: Twenty-first regnal year of Indrapala; approximately 1071 CE in Sharma’s chronology.
Ruler: Indrapala, Kamarupa Pala dynasty.
Place: Guwakuchi near Nalbari; discovered in 1925.

SUMMARY
The charter grants land yielding 2,000 units of paddy to Devadeva, a Brahmin whose family is traced through Vasudeva and Somadeva to a settlement in Sravasti. Much of the genealogy and formal language is shared with Indrapala’s Gauhati charter; the beneficiary and transaction details differ.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Useful for the king’s regnal sequence, the ancestry claimed by Brahmin beneficiaries, and land-grant administration. It also supplies a parallel text for correcting damaged portions of the Gauhati plates.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 247-260.
