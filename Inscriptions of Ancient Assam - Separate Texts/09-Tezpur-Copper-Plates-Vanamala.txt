Tezpur Copper Plates Vanamala

Date: Middle of the ninth century CE.
Ruler: Vanamala or Vanamalavarmadeva, Salastambha line.
Place: Found near Tezpur.

SUMMARY
A royal copper-plate charter combines genealogical praise with a religious land donation. Its description of Haruppesvara evokes a prosperous royal centre and its Brahmaputra setting. Much of its formal text parallels Vanamala’s Parbatiya charter. Sharma relies on earlier published readings because the original plates were unavailable to him.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Useful for the dynasty’s genealogy, royal capital, religious patronage and the landscape of the Tezpur region. Its transmission through early copies means some readings require caution.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 149-167.
