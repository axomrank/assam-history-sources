Dubi Copper Plates Bhaskaravarman

Date: Early seventh century CE; Sharma places it approximately in the first quarter.
Ruler: Bhaskaravarman, Varman dynasty.
Place: Dubi, near Pathsala, in the area described as Kamrup district.

SUMMARY
A copper-plate charter gives an extensive royal genealogy and praise of Bhaskaravarman’s predecessors, followed by grant-related material. Five plates survive from an original set of six, so the transaction is incompletely preserved. Its narrative includes the earlier donation tradition associated with Bhutivarman.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
A major source for the Varman royal succession, queens, royal claims and the religious setting of land grants. Its account combines dynastic history with praise and mythical ancestry; the missing final plate limits reconstruction of the grant.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 64-86.
