Bargaon Copper Plates Ratnapala

Date: Twenty-fifth regnal year of Ratnapala; approximately 1035 CE in Sharma’s chronology.
Ruler: Ratnapala, Kamarupa Pala dynasty.
Place: Bargaon in the Tezpur region.

SUMMARY
Ratnapala grants land capable of yielding 2,000 units of paddy to the Brahmin Viradatta. The land lies in Lavukuti within the district called Trayodasagrama on the northern bank of the Brahmaputra. The charter supplies royal genealogy, praise, grant conditions and detailed boundaries.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
A principal source for the Kamarupa Pala dynasty and Ratnapala’s administration. Its fiscal description and place names illuminate agricultural settlement, although several ancient localities cannot be securely identified.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 206-226.
