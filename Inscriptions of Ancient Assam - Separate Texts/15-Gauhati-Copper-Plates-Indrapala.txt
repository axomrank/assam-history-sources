Gauhati Copper Plates Indrapala

Date: Eighth regnal year of Indrapala; approximately 1058 CE in Sharma’s chronology.
Ruler: Indrapala, grandson and successor of Ratnapala in the Kamarupa Pala line.
Place: Barpanari in the Pati-Darrang area of the Guwahati subdivision, as described in the book.

SUMMARY
The king grants a plot assessed as yielding 4,000 units of paddy to the Brahmin Dehapala. The land is located in Hapyoma visaya on the northern bank of the Brahmaputra. The charter includes genealogy, praise, privileges and boundaries. Its damaged readings can sometimes be checked against the Guwakuchi charter.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Evidence for Pala succession, Brahmin patronage, land assessment and northern-bank administration. The title Gauhati identifies the published grant and should not be mistaken for an exact findspot within the modern city.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 233-246.
