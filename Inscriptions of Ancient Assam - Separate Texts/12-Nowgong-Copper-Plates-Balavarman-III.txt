Nowgong Copper Plates Balavarman III

Date: Eighth regnal year of Balavarman III; late ninth century CE.
Ruler: Balavarman III, Salastambha line.
Place: Nowgong, now generally spelled Nagaon.

SUMMARY
This charter records the donation of land called Hamsava in the Dijjina district on the southern bank, assessed as yielding 4,000 units of paddy. It describes the beneficiary, informs relevant officials and residents, and marks boundaries using roads, trees and ponds. Its genealogy and praise largely parallel the Uttarbarbil charter.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Useful for land assessment, administrative geography and the practical recording of boundaries. Together with the king’s other grants, it helps establish the relative sequence of his reign.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 196-205.
