Gachtal Copper Plates Gopalavarman

Date: Approximately 1080 CE in Sharma’s chronology.
Ruler: Gopalavarmadeva, son and successor of Indrapala.
Place: Gachtal near Dabaka, in the area described as Nowgong district; discovered in 1966.

SUMMARY
Two surviving plates record a grant by Gopalavarmadeva of land capable of yielding 8,000 units of paddy. The donated land is named Khirikoukonci in Barasrojambubila visaya in the edition’s reading. The final plate and seal are missing, so the beneficiary’s name and concluding details are unavailable.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Important direct evidence for Gopala’s place in the Pala succession and for the administrative geography of his reign. It must be distinguished from other records associated with the name Gachtal.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 261-278.
