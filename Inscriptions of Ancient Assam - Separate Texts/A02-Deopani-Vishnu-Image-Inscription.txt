Deopani Vishnu Image Inscription

Date: Eighth or ninth century CE; palaeographic datings differ.
Ruler: No securely identified issuing ruler in this record.
Place: Deopani; a standing stone Vishnu image discussed as preserved in the Assam State Museum.

SUMMARY
Four lines on the back of a Vishnu image contain a difficult religious text. Sharma tentatively interprets it as invoking Shiva, discussing the Goddess in relation to Vishnu, referring to worship by different social groups and identifying the image with Narayana. Part of the right-hand edge is lost.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Evidence for interaction among Shaiva, Vaishnava and Goddess traditions. The reading and interpretation are tentative, so detailed doctrinal conclusions should be treated cautiously.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 360-363.
