Parbatiya Copper Plates Vanamalavarmadeva

Date: Middle of the ninth century CE.
Ruler: Vanamalavarmadeva, Salastambha line.
Place: Parbatiya, near Tezpur.

SUMMARY
The charter grants the village of Haposu to a Brahmin and records the beneficiary’s family, the royal genealogy and the religious merit sought for the king and his parents. It also describes the royal centre of Haruppesvara and its setting. The text has substantial parallels with the Tezpur plates of the same king.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Provides evidence for land donation, Brahmin patronage and the royal centre in the Tezpur region. Comparing the two Vanamala charters helps identify shared formulas and differences specific to each transaction.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 168-180.
