Ulubari Copper Plates Balavarman III

Date: Thirteenth regnal year of Balavarman III; late ninth century CE.
Ruler: Balavarman III, Salastambha line.
Place: Ulubari village in Naharbari mauza, Darrang region; discovered in 1977.

SUMMARY
A three-plate charter grants land to Bhayadeva, son of Budhadeva and grandson of Vishnudeva. Its royal seal and much of its formal text resemble the Uttarbarbil and Nowgong grants. Sharma incorporates the newly discovered charter in the appendix and discusses its readings and grant formula.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Extends the documented regnal sequence of Balavarman III from the fifth and eighth years to the thirteenth. It supplies additional evidence for Brahmin patronage, land-grant practice and the dynasty’s documentary conventions.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 367-371.
