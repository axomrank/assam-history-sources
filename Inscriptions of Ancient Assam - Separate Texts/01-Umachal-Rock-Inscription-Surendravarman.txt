Umachal Rock Inscription Surendravarman

Date: Fifth century CE; no explicit calendar date.
Ruler: Surendravarman; his identification with a ruler of the Varman genealogy is debated.
Place: Umachal, on the Kamakhya hill at Guwahati.

SUMMARY
A short Sanskrit rock inscription records that Maharajadhiraja Surendravarman caused a cave shrine for the deity Balabhadra to be made. It commemorates a religious construction rather than a land grant.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
It is early direct evidence of royal religious patronage, Sanskrit writing and the eastern Gupta script in the Guwahati area. The ruler’s name is secure, but equating him with another similarly named king is an interpretation.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 55-57.
