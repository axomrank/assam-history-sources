Nagajari Khanikargaon Fragmentary Stone Inscription

Date: Early fifth century CE according to Sharma; other scholars proposed later dates.
Ruler: No identifiable reigning king survives.
Place: Nagajari-Khanikargaon, Sarupathar area, Golaghat region.

SUMMARY
A fragmentary stone inscription preserves portions of a record apparently concerning land and boundaries. Names including Brahmadatta and Vaji occur, but their roles are debated. Sharma considers them possible officials, witnesses or boundary references rather than securely identified recipients.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Potentially among the earliest epigraphic evidence from Assam. Its fragmentary condition prevents a complete reconstruction of the transaction, donor or ruler; the date is based on script rather than an explicit surviving year.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 357-359.
