Hayunthal Copper Plates Harjaravarman

Date: Middle of the ninth century CE.
Ruler: Harjaravarman, Salastambha line.
Place: Hayunthal, described in the book as formerly in Nowgong and subsequently in the Mikir Hills district.

SUMMARY
The surviving copper-plate material contains royal genealogy and praise connected with Harjaravarman. It recounts rulers of the Salastambha line and a narrative explaining their association with the term Mleccha. The document is incomplete, restricting recovery of the practical grant details.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
An important source for reconstructing the early Salastambha succession and the dynasty’s explanation of its origins. Its genealogical praise needs to be distinguished from independently established chronology.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 143-148.
