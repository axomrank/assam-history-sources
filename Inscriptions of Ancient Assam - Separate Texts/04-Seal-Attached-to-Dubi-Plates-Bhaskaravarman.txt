Seal Attached to Dubi Plates Bhaskaravarman

Date: Seventh century CE, associated with Bhaskaravarman’s Dubi charter.
Ruler: Bhaskaravarman and his Varman predecessors.
Place: Attached to the Dubi copper-plate grant.

SUMMARY
The seal has an elephant emblem and an inscription tracing the royal line from Pushyavarman through successive rulers and queens to Bhaskaravarman. It also asserts ancestry connected with Naraka, Bhagadatta and Vajradatta, and mentions horse sacrifices performed by earlier kings.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
It authenticates the charter and provides a compact genealogy useful for comparison with the Dubi plates and Nalanda seals. The mythical ancestry expresses royal legitimacy and is not independent proof of biological descent.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 87-88.
