Subhankarapataka Copper Plates Dharmapala

Date: Third regnal year of Dharmapala; first half of the twelfth century in this edition.
Ruler: Dharmapala, Kamarupa Pala dynasty.
Place: Exact findspot not established in the account; named after the donated locality.

SUMMARY
The grant concerns Subhankarapataka in Dijjina visaya, including specified additional land. Its total yield is assessed at 6,000 units of paddy, divided between the brothers Himanga and Trilocana in shares of 4,000 and 2,000. Their family background and connections with Sravasti are described.

HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE
Shows how a royal grant could be divided between related beneficiaries. It is useful for land assessment, Brahmin family history, administrative geography and the early regnal sequence of Dharmapala.

Source: M. M. Sharma, Inscriptions of Ancient Assam (1978), supplied PDF pages 295-308.
