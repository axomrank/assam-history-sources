Latitude is column E and longitude is column F. Coordinates are decimal degrees, north/east positive.
present_place describes the modern geographical findspot or its broad locality, not current museum custody.
coordinate_reference names the actual location represented by each coordinate. It may be a monument, a village, a nearby town, or a broader regional reference.
Approximate nearby references are not exact inscription recovery positions. Broad regional references may be many kilometres from an unresolved village; proximity is NOT claimed. Filter coordinate_type before making a detailed archaeological map.
coordinate_precision, location_source and location_notes identify the approximation and supporting source. No surveyed uncertainty radius is available for these fallback points.
